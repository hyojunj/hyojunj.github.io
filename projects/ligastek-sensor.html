Overview

During a summer internship at LigasTek, I worked on hardware that helps factories monitor their machines and working conditions.

Sensor circuit

I developed an Arduino sensor circuit that measures temperature and humidity. It was meant to be distributed to factories to improve machine efficiency and safety.

Enclosures

I designed, prototyped and tested cases for the PCBs and other hardware.

Energy harvesting

I improved the energy harvesting module so it could collect data and display the output in MATLAB.
