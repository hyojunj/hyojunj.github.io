Universal Steering Wheel Armature

One magnesium steering wheel armature designed to work across multiple vehicle platforms.

Date
2024–2026
Where
General Motors
Tools
LS-DYNA · OptiStruct · ANSA
Key figure
1st mode above 120 Hz

Overview

Alongside my main role supporting vehicle programs with interior simulations, I led the rapid development of a universal steering wheel armature at General Motors: a single cast magnesium core meant to be shared across vehicle platforms instead of designed per program.

Modal frequency

With the column base fixed, I extracted the armature's natural frequencies and mode shapes. The first mode had to stay above 120 Hz to keep the wheel clear of vehicle vibration, and the design met the target in the vertical, lateral and rim modes.

Rim bending strength

I simulated pushing the rim 65 mm at the 12 o'clock position and at 6 o'clock on a 30° fixture, then checked stress and plastic strain against the material's fracture limits. Both load cases met the requirement with no fracture, and the results drove the design iterations.

Test correlation

As the interior CAE point of contact for two high-priority programs, I worked with suppliers to correlate the CAE models with physical test data and reviewed their test setups on site.
