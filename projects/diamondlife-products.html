DiamondLife Product Design

SolidWorks models and photorealistic renders of display products, plus jigs and fixtures for the shop floor.

Date
2020–2021
Where
DiamondLife
Tools
SolidWorks · Rendering
Key figure
Renders used on the company website

Overview

As a part-time mechanical design engineer at DiamondLife, I designed new products, improved existing ones and made the company's manufacturing steps easier to repeat.

Product design and renders

I modeled the products in SolidWorks and turned the models into photorealistic renders that the company published on its website, including rolling display racks and aluminum slatwall inserts.

Manufacturing

On the shop side, I designed jigs and fixtures and built them into the manufacturing process to make production faster and more consistent.
