Overview

CMU Moon Miners is building an autonomous rover that digs and moves lunar regolith for the 2027 NASA Lunabotics Challenge. I'm on the structures team, contributing to the rover's structural design as it moves through preliminary and critical design reviews.

Structural analysis

I run FEA on structural components to verify their strength and stiffness under excavation loads, vibration, and driving over harsh, dusty regolith terrain.
